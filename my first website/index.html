Croissants

The buttery, flaky croissant is a pastry with a surprisingly non-French origin.
It is believed to have evolved from the Austrian pastry known as the "kipferl," which dates back to the 13th century.
This crescent-shaped delicacy made its way to France in the 19th century, where French bakers adapted and refined it into the layered masterpiece we know and love today.
